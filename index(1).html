The brain we are building NOT CONNECTED

Step 1 — Trend: Is price generally moving up, down, or sideways?
Step 2 — Momentum: Is the move gaining or losing strength?
Step 3 — Volume: Is trading activity supporting the move?
Step 4 — Risk: Where should the stop-loss go, and is the possible reward worth the risk?
Step 5 — Decision: BUY, SELL or WAIT. If the evidence disagrees, choose WAIT.
Real technical indicators and decision rules are not connected yet. All BUY/SELL signals and trade plans remain DEMO ONLY until those checks are implemented and validated against market data.
Market Scout is a decision-support prototype, not financial advice and not an automatic trading system.
